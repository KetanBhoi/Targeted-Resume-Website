---
name: AI resume verification
description: Why generated resume revisions remain drafts until the owner checks their claims.
---

Treat AI-tailored resumes as unverified drafts until the owner explicitly checks the complete change and confirms the facts. Do not automatically treat a generated copy as ready for applications or as a trusted source for further generation.

**Why:** Text diffs and checks for new numbers or names can catch some unsupported claims, but cannot prove every implication in a rewritten sentence is true. Automatic generation must not silently promote a plausible-sounding claim into the user's professional record.

**How to apply:** Show a complete before/after comparison, make the review status visible, and enforce the restriction on the server for downstream use of linked drafts. Keep the original version intact so review and restoration remain possible.
